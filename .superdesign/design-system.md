# Crack inspection design brief
Audience: research reviewers and people inspecting surface photographs.
Palette: background #0b0b0b, panels #111111, text #f2f0eb, muted #a4a4a4, rules #303030, accent #d62b2b.
Use zero-radius edges throughout. No purple, gradients, emoji, pill badges, decorative rounded panels, or fabricated metrics.
Layout: strong title and compact research identifier, upload / annotated result workspace, clear run action, readable per-region results, details beneath.
Keep detection, independent region gating, real language verification, and uncertainty disclosures intact.
Typography: system sans for readable text, monospace for measurements; restrained uppercase labels.
