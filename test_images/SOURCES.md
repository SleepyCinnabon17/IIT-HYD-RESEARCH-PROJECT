The official split was acquired from the dataset URL in Ultralytics' `crack-seg.yaml`; the downloaded ZIP is 96,089,865 bytes. `clear_01` and `clear_03` were chosen reproducibly by auditing the first 20 lexicographically ordered test images with non-empty labels and ranking `calibrated confidence - 3 × TTA standard deviation`. The complete pool and ranking are preserved in `evidence/official_test_candidate_audit.json`.

The earlier Commons-derived clear fixtures are retained under `difficult_cases/` because visual review and corrected TTA showed unstable or irrelevant top detections. Their original photographs remain in `source_images/`. The synthetic fixture is only a reproducible negative/ambiguity probe; it is not real-world evidence.
